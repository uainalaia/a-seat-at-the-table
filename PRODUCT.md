# A Seat at the Table

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
User-approved Astro, TypeScript, React islands, scoped CSS, GSAP, conditionally loaded Three.js, static Vercel hosting.

## Users
University of Arizona students with little or no experience at professional meals.

## Product Purpose
An English-language educational experience: receive an invitation, learn six chapters, explore a place setting, practice a recruiting dinner, and reflect on learning.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Original guided 3D dining room and seventeen-item equivalent SVG map, six-decision deterministic scenario with three options per step, ten visible survey questions (six knowledge, two confidence, two reflection), local in-memory answers. Original synthesis with contextual citations. Follow the supplied SIE 415/515 assignment and 100-point rubric. No student responses are collected. One primary implementation agent; no agent infrastructure. The user authorized installation of emil-design-eng and design-taste-frontend for the redesign.

## Brand Commitments
Approved name, sophisticated invitation and dining editorial, ivory, burgundy and charcoal, cinematic photography, Cormorant Garamond and Source Sans 3. Explicitly approved in the implementation request; do not reopen these decisions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard and touch access, reduced motion, readable static educational content, cultural context and respectful guidance. Student project, not an official university website.
